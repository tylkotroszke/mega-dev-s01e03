0002 · Proposed: deduplicate by supported cause

owner Mateusz Sawczuk proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Context

The review of #4471 finds different date problems hidden in one ticket. The old subject-only rule can merge different causes and split one cause across titles. Its interactive example already evaluates causes rather than subjects. The desk handles more than one product, so a date-specific classifier cannot safely classify every incoming report.

Proposed replacement for decision 0001

Use subject text to find candidates, then read the bodies and compare product, workflow, tenant configuration, reproduction and the fix needed. Merge only when those facts support the same cause. Unknown configuration or inconsistent symptoms require triage, not a blind merge. Retain the report IDs, first-received dates, channel and distinct-report count in each ticket.

For this exported date incident, tenant locale, booking form and channel help distinguish formatting mechanisms. The exported convention counts desk-noted temporal observations in their assigned group and preserves them in a footnote. Keep that distinction: an observation is not automatically a separate cause, and a routing label does not prove every report’s mechanism. Seek evidence before merging or separating work. Refresh evidence before acting on the live desk.

Consequences and status

This costs more reading at intake and avoids hiding a second fix behind “known”. Different subjects may merge when the evidence supports one cause; identical subjects may stay separate. Preserve decision 0001 unchanged. This proposal replaces it only after desk-owner approval; then the owner should update the operational triage page to match and review it again when the product, configuration or evidence changes.
